    Rules implemented, and what is verified vs. assumed
    
    
      
  • Verified from your photo: the four Start areas and their clockwise order (yellow top-left, green top-right, red bottom-right, blue bottom-left), with each color's Home and Safety Zone arm.
    • 
      
  • Assumed, not verified: the exact slide positions. The code uses four slides, one per side, each four squares long, with heads at track squares 5, 20, 35 and 50 (counting from yellow's Start as square 0). Check these against your physical board; they are in SLIDE_HEADS.
    • 
      
  • Assumed, not verified: 60 track squares, 5 Safety Zone squares plus Home per color, and that a pawn's lap ends on the square just before its own Start.
    • 
      
  • Standard 45-card deck: five 1s; four each of 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 and Sorry!. The discard pile is reshuffled when the deck runs out.
    • 
      
  • 1 or 2 brings a pawn out of Start, unless your own pawn is already on your Start square. Drawing a 2 always gives an extra draw.
    • 
      
  • Landing on an opponent on the main track sends that pawn back to Start. Pawns in Safety Zones or Home can't be bumped. You can't land on your own pawn.
    • 
      
  • Slides: a pawn that lands on a slide head (by a move of 1 to 12, a backward move, or a split of 7) slides to the tail four squares later, bumping any opponent on the tail. If your own pawn is on the tail, or the slide would run past your last main-track square, the slide isn't taken. Slides don't apply to Sorry! or swap landings.
    • 
      
  • 4 moves back 4. 10 moves forward 10 or back 1. 7 moves one pawn 7 or splits it between two pawns. 11 moves forward 11 or swaps with an opponent's main-track pawn (including one on its Start square).
    • 
      
  • Sorry! takes a pawn from your Start and sends an opponent's main-track pawn back to Start. If you have no pawn in Start or no target, the card is discarded.
    • 
      
  • If no move is legal, the card is discarded. Pawns in a Safety Zone can only move backward within it. Home holds any number of pawns.
    • 
      
  • Every player uses the same decision rule: maximize (your progress value − 0.7 × each opponent's progress value), with random tie-breaks. Results therefore measure turn order and seating, not one player playing better.
    • 
      
  • Turn order is clockwise around the board, starting from the selected player that comes first in seat order (yellow, green, red, blue). Unused colors still have their Start squares on the track.
    • 
      
  • Games stop after 3,000 player turns and count as "no winner."
